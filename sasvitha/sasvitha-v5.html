Get to Know Us

Chairman's Message

Sasvitha Home Finance primarily caters to the housing finance needs of self-employed groups and small-scale business owners, particularly in semi-urban and rural areas. These people live in urban neighbourhoods and small towns with aspirations for first-time home ownership, predominantly among cash-salaried or self-employed lower and middle income groups. Sasvitha Home Finance supports these first-time home buyers and those seeking home improvements by making finance accessible and affordable.
